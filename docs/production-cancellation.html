Cancelling a Production

How to call off a planned production and return its ingredients to stock

Applies to the Productions module · Version 1.0

1. What this is for

When you plan a production, the system immediately reserves the ingredients it will need, so they can't be double-booked by another plan. If that production then doesn't happen — the oven broke, staff didn't show, the day was a public holiday — those ingredients would otherwise stay locked up against a plan that will never be made.

Cancelling releases them. The moment you cancel, every ingredient reserved for that production goes straight back into available stock and can be used by another production, the same day.

Cancelling is not deleting The record stays in the batch, clearly marked as cancelled, showing who cancelled it, when, and why. Nothing disappears from your history.

2. Who can cancel

Cancelling requires the can_cancel_production permission. If you don't have it, the Cancel buttons simply won't appear. An administrator can grant it under Settings → Roles.

3. Cancelling one product from a batch

Use this when most of the batch went ahead but one item didn't get made.

  1. Go to Productions and find the batch.
  2. Click View Batch Details.
  3. In the Products in Batch table, find the product that wasn't made.
  4. Click the red Cancel button on that row.
  5. Type a short reason (optional but recommended — it's saved for the record).
  6. Click Cancel & release ingredients.

The row immediately shows a grey Cancelled tag, and that product's ingredients are back in stock.

4. Cancelling a whole batch

Use this when the entire day's production was called off.

  1. Go to Productions and find the batch.
  2. Click View Batch Details.
  3. Click Cancel Batch at the top right of the dialog.
  4. Type a reason and confirm.

Every remaining product in the batch is cancelled together and all their ingredients are released at once. The batch status becomes Cancelled.

All-or-nothing If even one product in the batch can't be cancelled (see Section 5), the whole action is refused and nothing is cancelled. This prevents a batch ending up half-cancelled. Cancel the eligible items individually instead.

5. When cancelling is not allowed

Cancellation is only for plans that never happened. The system will refuse in the situations below and explain why on screen.

Situation What you'll see What to do instead
Actual quantities already recorded "…cannot be cancelled: actual production has already been recorded for it." The goods physically exist, so this isn't a plan any more. Use Add / Edit Actual Production to correct the quantities.
Output already transferred, sold or given out "…its output has already been used by transfer #x, sale #y." Reverse or delete those transfers/sales first, then cancel.
Already cancelled "…is already cancelled." Nothing to do — it's done.
Why produced items can't be cancelled Once actual production is recorded, real bread exists and may already have moved to an outlet. Cancelling would remove stock the shops are relying on. Correcting the actual quantities is the safe way to fix a mistake there.

6. What changes after cancelling

WhereWhat you'll notice
Ingredient stock Reserved ingredients are available again immediately.
Batch Details The product stays listed with a Cancelled tag. Hover it to see who cancelled it, when, and the reason.
Batch totals & status Cancelled products no longer count towards the batch. A batch whose remaining products are all produced correctly shows Completed instead of being stuck on Pending.
Productions list Cancelled items are hidden from the main list — Batch Details is where their history remains visible.
Audit Log Records the cancellation with the user, time and reason.
Editing A cancelled product can no longer be edited or given actual quantities.

7. Common questions

Can I undo a cancellation?

No. If the production does go ahead after all, add it again as a new product in the batch (or as a new batch) — the ingredients are back in stock, so it will reserve them again normally.

What if only part of the planned quantity was made?

Don't cancel. Record the actual production with the quantity that was really made — that's exactly what actual quantities are for. Cancelling is only for items where nothing was produced.

The Cancel button isn't showing.

Check in this order:

  • Do you have the can_cancel_production permission?
  • Has the item already had actual quantities recorded? (It will show Produced.)
  • Is it already cancelled?

If you were granted the permission while already logged in, log out and back in — permissions are read at login.

Does cancelling affect the ingredients' cost or purchase records?

No. It only removes the reservation against those purchases. The purchase records, quantities and prices are untouched, and the ingredients return to the same purchase lots they were taken from.
